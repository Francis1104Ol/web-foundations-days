About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight productivity tool designed to help you instantly log your thoughts and task items. It acts as a rapid digital scratchpad ensuring that no brilliant insight or crucial reminder slips away during your busy workday.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Navigate to the Home panel interface.
  2. Type your task or thought into the "New note" text field.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button to dynamically append it to your active dashboard list.

Features

  • Instant local DOM rendering
  • Clean, minimalist markup foundation
  • Dynamic live note counter tracking
